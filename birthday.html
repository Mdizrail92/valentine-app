Forever with you 💕 • 6th Nov
My Dearest Dita,

Happy Birthday to the most extraordinary woman I have ever had the privilege of knowing — an aerospace engineer who builds plane navigators by day, and talks to a boy from Mumbai until 1am by night. 😄

I remember the first night we spoke — it was during Ramadan, you had just come home from work, and even though we were strangers, it felt like talking to someone I had always known. You told me about Batam, about Curtiss-Wright, about your room that "sees all of you" — your tears, your laughter, your silence. I felt honoured you let me in.

You have shown me what real strength looks like. Going to the emergency room alone at 3am. Boarding a flight to your mum's house for the first time in 2 years, crying the moment you saw her. Carrying the grief of losing your Daddy — and still waking up every morning to fight. You once said "I just have me." Dita, please know — you also have me. 🤲

You are the chemical engineer who reads books, keeps two pillows on her bed, loves chicken so much it is almost a personality, wants a café of her own one day, and dreams of Switzerland from Indian movies and songs. You are uniquely, beautifully you.

I pray that this new year of your life brings you the house in Batam you are saving for, a job in Singapore if that is where your heart wants to go, and most of all — someone who will be right beside you at 3am, so you never have to call an Ojek alone again.

And Bali — Inshaallah, one day we will stand at that gate together. 🌴

Happy Birthday, Ditaaa. The world is brighter because you are in it. 🎂✨

Forever your friend (and the boy who saved your contact as "Dita ❤️"), Israil ❤️
